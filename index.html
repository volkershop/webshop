  • Once payment has been confirmed and the ordered work has been successfully completed/delivered, no full or partial refund is provided, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • If an eligible Resource service falls within the warranty terms, the available remedy is handled through the shop's claim system, such as a free redo or an equal-price replacement service, rather than a cash refund.
  • Before payment, customers should verify the game, service, price, compatibility and required information. Contact the admin before paying if anything is unclear.

  • ถ้าเป็นบริการประเภท Resource และบัญชีถูกแบนภายใน 7 วันหลังงาน completed สามารถใช้สิทธิ์เคลมได้สูงสุด 2 ครั้งต่อบริการ

4. Resource-service warranty

  • Eligible Resource services include a 7-day warranty after the job is marked completed, with up to 2 claim credits per service.
  • Each credit can be used for either a free redo or a switch to another game's service at exactly the same price.
  • Claims are tied to Customer ID + Order ID + Service ID to prevent duplicate/excess claims.
  • Admin may verify claim details and reject claims that do not match the order or fall outside the warranty period.
